12, 500, 000. The algorithm discovered 704 cases whose analysis shows that the
distribution of the values of k/√x ∈ (0, 16] is very close to a uniform distribution
for this range of k (the mean is 7.996 and the Kolmogorov-Smirnov test gives a
conﬁdence of 98.60% to the hypothesis of a uniform distribution). T his gives extra
support to the possibility that the size of k/√x may be arbitrarily small, and so
the corresponding exponent in Hall’s conjecture must be at most 1 /2 − ǫ. The data
also support that the number of cases for x < X and k ≤ n√x may be estimated
as 0 .80 n log(X), somewhat less than the expected value n log(X).
