Let $f(z)=\prod_{i=1}^n(z-z_i)\in \mathbb{C}[z]$ with $\lvert z_i\rvert < 1$ for all $i$. Must there always exist a path of length less than $2$ in\[\{z: \lvert f(z)\rvert < 1\}\]which connects two of the roots of $f$? #1041 : [EHP58,p.139] analysis | polynomials A problem of Erdős, Herzog, and Piranian [EHP58] , who proved that this set always has a connected component containing at least two of the roots.
