They also suggest the stronger conjecture that $\limsup 1_A\ast 1_A(n)/\log n>0$. Another stronger conjecture would be that the hypothesis $\lvert A\cap [1,N]\rvert \gg N^{1/2}$ for all large $N$ suffices. See also [40] , and [1145] for a stronger generalisation. This is discussed in problem C9 of Guy's collection [Gu04] .
