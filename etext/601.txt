In [Er82e] Erdős offers \$250 for showing what happens when $\alpha=\omega_1^{\omega+2}$ and \$500 for settling the general case. Larson [La90] proved this is true for all $\alpha<2^{\aleph_0}$ assuming Martin's axiom .
